# Scandi Warm Minimal

Adjacent to Swiss minimalism but warmed considerably — closer to Copenhagen interiors than Zurich poster design. Palette borrows from natural materials: oak, oat, linen, charcoal. Humanist sans (not geometric) for warmth in the letterforms. Whitespace generous but lived-in rather than clinical.

## Principles
- Warmth in the ground — never pure white.
- Humanist sans rather than geometric.
- Earth-toned accents — rust, sage, oat, terracotta.
- Generous whitespace; line-height on the high side.
- One material texture per view, used full-bleed.

## Typography
- Söhne, Inter, Atlas Grotesk, or Larsseit at 400/500/600.
- Body 16-17px with 1.65 line-height.
- Display 1.4-1.6× body at weight 600.
- Tracking neutral on body, -0.015em on display.
- Numerals tabular for data, oldstyle for prose.

## Color Palette
- Ground: `oklch(96% 0.02 80)` (warm cream)
- Body: `oklch(30% 0.02 60)`
- Surface: `oklch(92% 0.03 80)` (sand)
- Accent: `oklch(55% 0.10 60)` (restrained terracotta)
- Muted: `oklch(60% 0.02 60)`
- Hairline: `oklch(80% 0.02 60)`

## Layout Rules
- 8-column grid marketing, 12-column utility.
- Sections separated by 5-7rem or hairline.
- Photography colour-graded warm.
- Material references (linen, oak) sparingly as accent texture.
- No card backgrounds; surfaces lift via ground-tone shifts.

## Anti-Slop Boundaries
- No purple-blue gradient — palette is earth-toned.
- No glassmorphism — antithetical to material-honest.
- No gradient text — warmth lives in restraint.

## NEVER
- Geometric sans (kills the warmth).
- Pure-white ground.
- Saturated accents (cap at 0.12 chroma).
- Drop shadows above 1 unit blur.
