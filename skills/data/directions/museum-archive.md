# Museum Archive

Modelled on the metadata grammar of museum wall-text and exhibition catalogs. Information organised by hierarchy and convention: artist, date, medium, dimensions. Relies on serif body, small-caps labels, hairlines, ivory ground. Reads as curated rather than designed.

## Principles
- Ivory ground tightly controlled.
- Metadata grammar — labels small-caps, values serif.
- Hairline rules at low chroma; no borders or boxes.
- Numerals oldstyle for prose, tabular for dimensions.
- Information density welcome — look is catalog, not marketing.

## Typography
- Body: GT Sectra, Tiempos Text, or Charter at 15-16px.
- Labels: same family at 0.85em, small-caps, +0.06em tracking.
- Display: same family at weight 500, no bolder.
- Italics for titles of works.
- Numerals oldstyle prose, lining tabular for data.

## Color Palette
- Ground: `oklch(96% 0.015 85)`
- Body: `oklch(22% 0.01 60)`
- Rule: `oklch(78% 0.01 60)`
- Muted: `oklch(50% 0.01 60)`
- Accent: `oklch(35% 0.10 25)` (deep oxblood, rare emphasis)

## Layout Rules
- Three-column grid with wide outer gutters.
- Metadata left, primary content right.
- Hairlines between every catalog entry.
- Images quarter-column or full-bleed; nothing in-between.
- Captions below images in small-caps + italic.

## Anti-Slop Boundaries
- No gradient text — defeats the catalog metaphor.
- No card-with-shadow — uses hairlines and whitespace.
- No ornamental flourishes — small-caps + serif IS the ornament.

## NEVER
- Sans-serif body.
- Drop shadows or 3D effects.
- Saturated accent (cap at 0.10 chroma).
- Photography without caption + metadata.
