# Bauhaus Geometric

Descends directly from Dessau — Itten, Albers, Bayer. Design as construction: form follows function, geometry is honest, ornament is dishonest. Primary triad plus achromatic; geometric sans built from circles and rectangles; visible structural grid. The work reads as built rather than styled.

## Principles
- Primary triad only: red, yellow, blue, plus black and white.
- Geometry is honest — circles are circles, squares are squares.
- The grid is structural and often visible.
- Form follows function — every element earns its position.
- Typography built from primitive shapes.

## Typography
- Display: Futura, ITC Avant Garde, or Neue Haas Grotesk Display.
- Body: same families at regular weight.
- Numerals: lining, geometric.
- Tracking: tight on display (-0.02em), neutral on body.
- All-caps with +0.08em tracking for short labels only.

## Color Palette
- Red: `oklch(55% 0.20 25)`
- Yellow: `oklch(85% 0.18 90)`
- Blue: `oklch(45% 0.18 250)`
- Black: `oklch(15% 0 0)`
- White: `oklch(98% 0 0)`
- Colours used flat — no gradients, tints, or shades.

## Layout Rules
- Strict modular grid — 12-column or 8-column, visible if structural.
- Asymmetry preferred over symmetry; balanced via weight.
- Geometric shapes used as composition elements.
- One colour dominates per composition.
- Rules and hairlines are part of the grammar.

## Anti-Slop Boundaries
- No gradients — Bauhaus rejected gradient as dishonest.
- No decorative drop shadows.
- No softening primary triad toward pastels.

## NEVER
- Serifs (geometric sans only).
- Rounded corners on primary surfaces.
- Secondary colour ranges (triad is the system).
- Drop shadows or 3D effects.
