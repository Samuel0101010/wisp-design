# Archival Typewriter

Adopts the visual grammar of typewritten manuscripts and carbon-copy archives. Type is Courier-or-equivalent (typewriter monospace, not coding monospace); colour is faded ink against warm paper; spacing rhythm mimics double-spaced manuscript. Descends from screenwriting conventions and the contemplative-writing-app lineage (iA Writer, Ulysses).

## Principles
- Typewriter monospace — Courier Prime, JetBrains Mono Slab.
- Paper-tone ground — warm cream with slight yellow.
- Faded-ink body — never pure black; dark grey-brown.
- Generous line-height (1.7-1.8) mimics double-spaced manuscript.
- Wide unequal margins — left wider than right (manuscript convention).

## Typography
- Body: Courier Prime, Courier Final Draft, American Typewriter.
- Weights: 400 only — typewriters had no weight variation.
- Body 16-18px with 1.75 line-height.
- All-caps for headings with +0.05em tracking.
- Underlines welcome — typewriters underlined for emphasis.

## Color Palette
- Ground: `oklch(95% 0.025 85)` (warm paper)
- Body: `oklch(28% 0.03 50)` (faded brown-black ink)
- Muted: `oklch(50% 0.03 50)` (carbon-copy secondary)
- Accent: `oklch(38% 0.16 25)` (red-ink stamp, sparingly)
- Hairline: `oklch(75% 0.03 60)` (faint pencil rule)

## Layout Rules
- Single-column manuscript layout — 65ch max.
- Page-like vertical rhythm with clear page-break sections.
- Hairlines or `---` dashes as section separators.
- No cards, no surfaces — the page IS the surface.
- Numbered lines acceptable for scripts.

## Anti-Slop Boundaries
- No gradient text — typewriters print solid.
- No modern drop shadows — page is flat.
- No glassmorphism — antithetical to paper.
- No sans-serif body — kills the premise.

## NEVER
- Sans-serif typography anywhere.
- Pure-white ground.
- Saturated accent colours.
- Decorative ornament beyond hairlines and dashes.
