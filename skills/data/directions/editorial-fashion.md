# Editorial Fashion

Translates the Vogue/Harper's Bazaar/AnOther magazine-cover aesthetic into interface. Type is the entire show — extreme-contrast Didone serifs at display sizes, generous tracking, aggressive whitespace. Photography (when present) full-bleed and editorial-graded. The work reads as confident enough not to explain itself.

## Principles
- Display Didone or Bodoni-style serif at very large sizes.
- Pure-white ground — the rare case where pure white is correct.
- Generous tracking on all-caps display — +0.10em to +0.20em.
- Photography colour-graded high-contrast, often desaturated.
- Whitespace is the primary spatial element.

## Typography
- Display: Bodoni, Didot, GT Super, or Playfair at 60-120px.
- Body: Inter or Söhne for readability against the serif drama.
- Tracking +0.10em on all-caps display, neutral on body.
- Numerals oldstyle prose, tabular prices.
- Italics welcome in display for variation.

## Color Palette
- Ground: `oklch(99% 0 0)` (pure white earned)
- Body: `oklch(15% 0 0)` (high contrast)
- Muted: `oklch(55% 0 0)` (captions)
- Accent: none, or single saturated `oklch(45% 0.18 25)` deep red, sparingly.
- No surface tones; everything on pure white.

## Layout Rules
- Asymmetric — type oversized left, photography opposite.
- Full-bleed photography in single-column views; no cards.
- Aggressive vertical rhythm — 8-12rem between sections.
- Display headlines may break the grid intentionally.
- Captions all-caps + tracking, set very small (10-12px).

## Anti-Slop Boundaries
- No gradient text — the serif IS the design.
- No decorative flourishes — display Bodoni is ornament.
- No card backgrounds — surfaces stay on pure white.

## NEVER
- Drop shadows.
- Colour-pop saturated photography.
- Rounded corners on primary elements.
- Generic stock photography.
